## Why ~$500 Works

- **Above** the self-directed local options ($280–$400) — because she's offering instruction, curation, and a complete experience
- **In line with** Paper & i and Art & Contemplation — established retreats with loyal followings
- **Below** institutional programs (Arrowmont, Shenandoah) — approachable for first-timers
- **Better value** than DoArtWorx ($540 without lodging) and Ox-Bow ($600 without instruction)

This is the "I can say yes without agonizing" price point. A weekend away that feels special without feeling like a financial stretch. That's how you build a repeat audience.
